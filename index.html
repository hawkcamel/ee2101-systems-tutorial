    Before you start

    You need Ohm's law, KVL and KCL from DC circuits, and basic differentiation and integration. That's all. Complex numbers are covered from scratch in Lesson 1.

    Everything here assumes ideal components: no resistance in the wires, no leakage in the capacitors. Real parts behave a bit differently, and the lab sessions are where you'll see how.
