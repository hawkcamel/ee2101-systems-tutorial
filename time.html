03 / CONNECT THE EQUATIONS

One function. Two perspectives.

At s = σ + jω:
F(s) = A + jB = r ejθ = r ∠ θ

r = √(A² + B²), θ = atan2(B, A). For a factored function, multiply distances to zeros and divide by distances to poles; subtract pole angles from zero angles.

What does a unit step actually change?

The input u(t) = 1 for t ≥ 0 has transform U(s) = 1/s. Thus Y(s) = H(s)/s. It adds an origin pole to the unreduced output expression, not to H itself. Coincident poles and zeros may cancel. Adding 1/s to H is a different operation.
