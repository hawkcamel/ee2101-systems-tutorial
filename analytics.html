For the instructor: a future analytics setup

When you are ready, add authenticated accounts and a protected reporting service. A verified student ID—not an email typed into a form—should connect activity to a learner.

  1. Provision students through institutional login or email activation links. Let students choose a password; avoid using matric numbers as shared initial secrets.
  2. Record lesson/section IDs, start/end times, focused active seconds, interactive exercise attempts and completion events. Pause time when the tab is hidden or after an agreed idle timeout (for example 60 seconds).
  3. Send small authenticated event batches to a server with deduplication, server timestamps and per-course authorization. Do not embed student records or credentials in this public site.
  4. Give authorized instructors an aggregate section view and, where appropriate, a student detail view. Explain the collection to students, set retention rules, and restrict exports.
  5. Interpret time as an engagement estimate, not proof of reading or understanding. Combine it with short concept checks and student feedback.

This is a design outline, not an active analytics system. Login and analytics are postponed while we develop the lessons.
